Overview

Stranger Things is a science fiction mystery series set in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana. When a young boy mysteriously disappears, his friends and family begin searching for him. Their journey leads them to a mysterious girl named Eleven, who possesses extraordinary supernatural abilities. As they uncover secret experiments and a terrifying alternate dimension known as the Upside Down, the group faces dangerous creatures and unexpected mysteries. Filled with friendship, adventure, suspense and supernatural events, Stranger Things takes viewers on an unforgettable journey.
