Overview

Dark is a German science fiction mystery thriller series set in the small town of Winden, where the disappearance of a young boy reveals a mysterious connection between four families. As the investigation unfolds, the residents discover a hidden secret involving time travel and events that connect different generations. Their search for answers takes them through different timelines, uncovering unexpected relationships, secrets and shocking truths. Filled with suspense, complex storytelling and mind-bending mysteries, Dark explores the connection between time, fate and family.
