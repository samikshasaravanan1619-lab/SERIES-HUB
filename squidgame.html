Overview

Squid Game is a South Korean thriller series created by Hwang Dong-hyuk. The story follows Seong Gi-hun, a man facing financial difficulties who receives a mysterious invitation to participate in a secret competition. Along with hundreds of other participants, he enters a series of games inspired by traditional childhood activities. However, the competition is not what it seems. Each challenge tests the participants' decisions, trust, courage and relationships. As the competition progresses, hidden secrets about the organisation begin to emerge. With suspense, unexpected twists and powerful storytelling, Squid Game explores human nature, inequality and the value of life.
