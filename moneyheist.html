Overview

Money Heist, also known as La Casa de Papel, is a Spanish crime thriller series about a mysterious criminal mastermind known as The Professor. He plans an extraordinary robbery on the Royal Mint of Spain and brings together a group of skilled criminals, each using a city name as their identity. As the carefully planned operation unfolds, the team faces unexpected challenges, emotional conflicts and intense negotiations with the police. Filled with suspense, clever strategies, dramatic twists and unforgettable characters, Money Heist keeps viewers hooked until the end.
