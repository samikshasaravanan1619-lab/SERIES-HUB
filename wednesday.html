Overview

Wednesday follows the mysterious and intelligent Wednesday Addams as she begins her journey at Nevermore Academy, a school for supernatural students. While trying to master her emerging psychic abilities, she investigates a series of strange events and uncovers dark secrets connected to her family's past. Filled with mystery, supernatural adventures, dark humour and unexpected friendships, Wednesday takes viewers into a fascinating world of secrets and suspense.
